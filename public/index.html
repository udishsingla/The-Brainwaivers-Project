Official procedures, privacy compliance, and redressal mechanisms

Our cross-bureau overlap engine checks incoming grievances against existing dossiers using AICTE Permanent Institute IDs, category classification, and temporal proximity. When parallel inquiries are detected between the Regulation Bureau (RB) and Public Grievance Redressal Cell (PGRC), a joint inquiry alert is dispatched to both bureaus to avoid conflicting orders.
Under the AICTE Approval Process Handbook (APH), all formal institutional grievances are governed by a statutory 30-day Service Level Agreement (SLA). The system tracks the remaining timeline dynamically, triggering automated high-priority escalation if an inquiry remains unaddressed within 5 days of the deadline.
All personal identifiers (email, phone, student records) are encrypted at rest using AES-256 and salted hash algorithms. Complainant identities are shielded from institutions during initial scrutiny to protect students from victimization, strictly adhering to our comprehensive Privacy Policy.
Complainants may upload authenticated PDF dossiers, fee payment receipts, written communication from college administrators, or official inspection photos up to 10MB per submission.

AICTE Privacy Policy & Terms

1. Scope & Purpose: This portal processes grievance data solely to facilitate dispute resolution, investigate regulatory non-compliance, and prevent cross-bureau administrative overlaps as mandated by the AICTE Act, 1987.

2. Whistleblower Protection: The identity of student and faculty complainants is held strictly confidential and never disclosed to respondent institutions without explicit statutory legal process.

3. Data Security & Bloom Filter: Technical identifiers and hash keys are indexed in an in-memory Bloom filter for high-concurrency deduplication without storing plaintext keys in unencrypted caches.

4. Citizen Rights: Complainants possess statutory rights to review the audit trail, download certified dossier slips, and request correction of inaccurate records.
