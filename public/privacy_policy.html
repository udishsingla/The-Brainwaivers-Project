1. Introduction

Welcome to the AICTE Complaint Management Portal (“Portal”). The Portal is designed to facilitate the secure submission, assignment, monitoring and resolution of complaints submitted by students, faculty members, educational institutions and other stakeholders.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is collected, used, stored, processed and shared when users access the Portal and its services.

The Portal aims to handle personal information responsibly, maintain confidentiality and restrict access to authorised personnel in accordance with applicable laws and policies.

Note: If this website is a prototype or independently developed project, it must not be represented as an officially operated or approved AICTE service without authorisation.
2

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used, the Portal may collect the following categories of information:

2.1 Personal & Account
  • • Full name
  • • Email address
  • • Mobile number (if provided)
  • • Login credentials & auth data
  • • Role, institution or department
2.2 Complaint Information
  • • Title, category & description
  • • Relevant bureau or department
  • • Reference number & status
  • • Submission date & logs
  • • Communication records
  • • Uploaded attachments
2.3 Technical Data
  • • IP address
  • • Browser type & version
  • • Access timestamps
  • • System server logs

Important Recommendation: Users should avoid including unnecessary personal, financial, or highly sensitive confidential information in complaint descriptions or supporting attachments.

3

3. Purpose of Data Collection

Information collected through the Portal may be used to:

  • Register and authenticate users securely.
  • Receive and systematically record complaints.
  • Categorise and route complaints to appropriate bureaus.
  • Enable authorised officers to review and resolve grievances.
  • Provide complaint reference numbers and status updates.
  • Communicate requests for additional information.
  • Detect fraudulent submissions and unauthorized access.
  • Generate aggregated processing and trend reports.

Personal information should not be used for unrelated purposes unless permitted or required under applicable law.

4

4. Complaint Assignment and Bureau Access

The Portal assigns complaints to relevant bureaus, departments, or authorised officials according to category, subject matter, and administrative responsibilities. Access is strictly role-based:

Complainants

May access their own complaints and permitted status information.

Bureau Officers

May access complaints assigned to their bureau or role.

Supervisors

May review records and progress within their administrative scope.

Portal Administrators

May access information necessary to maintain the system under strict controls.

Complaint information must not be disclosed to unauthorised users. Any interdepartmental transfer is strictly limited to information necessary for handling the complaint.

5. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

No Commercial Exploitation We do not sell data for advertising

Information may be shared, where necessary and legally permitted, exclusively with:

  • The bureau or department responsible for resolving a complaint.
  • Authorised supervisory or administrative officials.
  • Approved technical service providers responsible for hosting, maintenance, notifications or security.
  • Competent public authorities where disclosure is required or authorized by applicable law.

6. Data Security and Confidentiality

The Portal implements appropriate technical and organizational safeguards against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure:

Secure HTTPS Connections
Password Hashing & Auth
Role-Based Access Control
Session Protection
Upload & File Validation
Administrative Audit Logs

Note: No digital system can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their login credentials and report suspected unauthorized access through designated support channels immediately.

7

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Personal information and complaint records are retained only for as long as necessary to fulfil their intended purposes, meet administrative requirements, resolve pending complaints, and maintain official records.

Retention periods are defined by the authorised data controller or responsible organization. Deletion or correction requests are evaluated in accordance with applicable legal obligations; records subject to mandatory preservation may not be deleted immediately.

8

8. Cookies and Session Management

The Portal uses essential cookies or browser session storage to maintain active logins, ensure session security, and enable core navigation features.

If analytics or non-essential tracking features are integrated in the future, their usage will be explained separately with appropriate opt-out choices. Users can manage cookies via browser preferences, though disabling essential cookies may impact Portal functions.

9. User Rights and Requests

Subject to applicable law, users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information, or raise concerns regarding processing.

Requests must be submitted through the Portal's designated privacy or grievance contact. Identity verification will be performed before processing any modification or disclosure request.

10. Third-Party Services

Approved third-party service providers may be used for hosting, email delivery, authentication, or technical maintenance. Such entities process data strictly to perform assigned functions under binding confidentiality terms.

External links on the Portal are governed by their respective privacy policies. Users should review third-party policies prior to disclosing personal data.

11. Children's Privacy

The Portal is not intended to collect unnecessary personal information from children. Where a complaint involves a minor or a minor submits information, statutory safeguards under relevant child protection and privacy laws are applied.
